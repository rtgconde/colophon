Colophon

Every figure in MoMA's exhibition catalogues, 1929 to 2007

284 scanned catalogues, 22,297 pages, 45,489 pictures, each one found and described by a model. Search by what a picture shows, by artist, title, medium or year; follow a work across the catalogues that reproduced it; open the page it was printed on.

How it was made

  1. SAM 3.1, Meta's open-weight segmentation model, boxes every picture on every page of the 757 catalogues MoMA gives away as PDFs: 77,750 pages, 93,527 figures, on three GPUs at home.
  2. Muse Spark, through the Meta Model API, reads each page with its boxes numbered and writes one record per figure: what it shows, the caption as printed, artist, title, year, medium, credit.
  3. The records become this index: the search, the works that recur across catalogues, and the page each figure came from. A second pass read the title pages and catalogued each book from its own evidence.
